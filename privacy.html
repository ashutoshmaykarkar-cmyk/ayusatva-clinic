WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy

This policy outlines the privacy practices and information handling for Ayusatva Ayurved & Panchkarma Clinic.

Direct clinic contact

This website connects you directly with the clinic via official phone and WhatsApp. This static website has no database or server storage and does not collect or process your personal health records online. When you call or send a message via WhatsApp or email, communications are handled directly between you and the clinic under strict professional confidentiality.

Contact information

Only basic contact and scheduling details are requested. Please do not write symptoms, diagnoses, prescriptions, test results or other sensitive health information in the scheduling note. If you send a message by WhatsApp or email, those providers and the clinic may handle the data according to their respective practices.

Cookies and third-party resources

No analytics, advertising trackers, or non-essential cookies are installed on this website. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts when network access is available; users' browsers connect to Google's font servers for this purpose. Locally available fallback fonts are used otherwise.

Questions and corrections
