Medical disclaimer

This website offers general educational information about traditional Ayurvedic concepts and possible areas of care. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, a guarantee of outcomes, or an invitation to perform clinical procedures without supervision.

Consult a qualified professional

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting new herbal preparations, dietary restrictions, or Panchakarma procedures—particularly if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, older, taking medicines, or have a chronic medical condition. Some interventions may have contraindications and adverse effects.

Emergency care

Do not use this website or electronic messaging for emergencies. Seek emergency medical services immediately for severe or sudden symptoms such as difficulty breathing, chest pain, stroke symptoms, heavy bleeding or loss of consciousness.

Prescribed treatment

Do not stop or alter prescribed medication or delay appropriate investigations or evidence-based care based on information presented here. Inform all your treating professionals about herbs, medicines and supplements you use.

Service availability

Descriptions of Panchakarma and consultations do not confirm that a specific procedure is provided at this clinic. Individual suitability, personalized therapy schedules, and clinical guidance are established directly during your personal consultation.
