01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Swedana.

Classical Ayurvedic plans may include Swedana in preparatory or supportive contexts. The method, duration and intensity are tailored to the individual and should not be confused with routine recreational sauna use.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

Understanding warm fomentation and its place in a supervised plan that may also involve oil-based therapy.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What a session may involve.

The following is a broad orientation, not a procedure manual. Exact methods differ across practitioners and patients.

01

Before the session

The practitioner checks medical suitability, heat tolerance and hydration concerns.

02

During the session

A specific fomentation method is selected and heat exposure is monitored throughout.

03

Aftercare & review

The session is stopped if discomfort occurs; cooling, hydration and rest guidance follow.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

Heat can cause burns, dizziness, dehydration or worsen some conditions. People with fever, heat intolerance, cardiovascular concerns, pregnancy, or reduced sensation should seek explicit medical assessment before a heat-based procedure.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Is Swedana always full-body steam?

No. Ayurveda describes different forms of fomentation, including localized methods.

Can I do it whenever I want?

Not necessarily. Heat-related procedures need screening and supervision, especially in people with medical conditions.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
