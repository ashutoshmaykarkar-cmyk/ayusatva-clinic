01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Nasya.

Nasya is a clinical procedure category in the Ayurvedic framework, not simply a daily self-care trend. Practitioner assessment governs what, if anything, is administered and when.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

Traditional head-and-neck-care terminology and the clinician’s assessment of suitability.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What a session may involve.

The following is a broad orientation, not a procedure manual. Exact methods differ across practitioners and patients.

01

Before the session

The clinician reviews nasal symptoms, respiratory history, medical conditions and current medications.

02

During the session

The preparation, quantity, timing and patient position are chosen in a controlled setting.

03

Aftercare & review

Post-procedure instructions and any need for follow-up are explained.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

Nasal administration may cause irritation, coughing or aspiration risk if performed incorrectly. Do not put unverified oils or herbal mixtures in the nose at home. Acute nasal or respiratory concerns need appropriate medical evaluation.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Can any oil be put into the nose?

No. Nasal products and procedures are not interchangeable and require professional judgment.

Is Nasya a substitute for ENT treatment?

No. Persistent nasal, breathing or ear, nose and throat symptoms need medical evaluation.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
