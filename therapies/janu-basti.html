01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Janu Basti.

Like other local oil-retention approaches, Janu Basti is often discussed in relation to comfort-focused therapies. It is not a replacement for diagnosis, rehabilitation or medical management of knee problems.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

Questions about external knee-region care and how to combine it with an appropriate assessment of movement concerns.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What a session may involve.

The following is a broad orientation, not a procedure manual. Exact methods differ across practitioners and patients.

01

Before the session

Knee symptoms, injury history, swelling, skin health and comfort are reviewed.

02

During the session

A suitable local setup is applied with cautious temperature control and monitoring.

03

Aftercare & review

The clinician discusses how the session fits, if at all, with activity guidance and follow-up.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

New severe swelling, redness, fever, trauma or inability to bear weight needs medical assessment. Heat or oil exposure may be unsuitable in some inflammatory, sensory or skin conditions.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Is Janu Basti an injection?

No. It is a localized external oil-retention practice, not an intra-articular injection.

Can it replace exercise or medical advice?

No. Depending on the cause, rehabilitation or other established medical care may be important.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
