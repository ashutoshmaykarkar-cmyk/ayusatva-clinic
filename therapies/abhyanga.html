Abhyanga

The classical art of herbal oil massage

01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Abhyanga.

In Ayurvedic practice, Abhyanga is part of a broader approach to daily and therapeutic oil application. A practitioner considers skin condition, comfort, physical health, and treatment goals before selecting a method.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

Dryness, a preference for calming body-care rituals, or questions about external therapies in a broader Ayurvedic care plan.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What a session may involve.

The following is a broad orientation, not a procedure manual. Exact methods differ across practitioners and patients.

01

Before the session

The practitioner reviews health concerns, skin sensitivity, allergies and comfort preferences.

02

During the session

An appropriate oil and method are selected; warm oil may be applied through gentle, measured strokes.

03

Aftercare & review

After the session, the practitioner advises on cleaning, rest, and whether any other therapy is appropriate.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

Oils can trigger skin reactions or cause discomfort if too hot. Open wounds, active skin infections, acute illness, or certain medical conditions may require postponement or modification. Do not assume a massage treats an underlying disease.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Is Abhyanga the same as Panchakarma?

No. Abhyanga is an oil-based external therapy; it is not by itself one of the classical principal Panchakarma procedures.

How is the oil chosen?

A practitioner considers the person’s needs, sensitivity, preferences and the intended care plan. An oil that suits one person may not suit another.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
