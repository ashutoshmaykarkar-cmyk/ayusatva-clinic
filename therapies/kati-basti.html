01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Kati Basti.

Kati Basti is a regional oil-retention practice, distinct from rectal Basti used in Panchakarma. It may be included in an individualized plan, but persistent back pain or neurological symptoms require proper assessment.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

Understanding how a localized external therapy may fit alongside movement advice and clinical evaluation.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What a session may involve.

The following is a broad orientation, not a procedure manual. Exact methods differ across practitioners and patients.

01

Before the session

The practitioner reviews back symptoms, warning signs, skin condition and heat tolerance.

02

During the session

A ring is positioned locally, and temperature-controlled oil is retained for an appropriate interval.

03

Aftercare & review

The area is cleaned, comfort assessed and broader care advice discussed.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

Hot oil can burn the skin. Back pain with new weakness, numbness, fever, injury or bladder/bowel changes needs timely medical assessment rather than relying on a surface therapy.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Is Kati Basti the same as Basti Panchakarma?

No. Kati Basti is an external oil-retention practice; classical Panchakarma Basti refers to administered enemas.

Will it cure slipped discs?

No such cure should be promised. Symptoms and underlying causes need medical evaluation and appropriate management.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
