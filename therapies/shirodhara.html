01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Shirodhara.

Shirodhara is known for its distinctive suspended vessel and steady flow. It may be discussed within relaxation-oriented Ayurvedic programs, but it should never be presented as a universal treatment for insomnia, anxiety or other illnesses.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

Questions about relaxation-focused care and traditional forehead oil-flow procedures.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What a session may involve.

The following is a broad orientation, not a procedure manual. Exact methods differ across practitioners and patients.

01

Before the session

A practitioner reviews medical background, sensitivities and whether the procedure fits the care plan.

02

During the session

The patient is positioned comfortably and the chosen liquid is delivered in a measured, supervised stream.

03

Aftercare & review

The area is cleaned and aftercare is discussed before the patient leaves.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

Temperature, skin and scalp sensitivity, infection risk and safe positioning matter. Avoid assuming that a relaxing experience is evidence of a proven medical benefit. People with acute illness or scalp conditions should seek individual advice.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Is Shirodhara a hair treatment?

The procedure involves the head and forehead, but its purpose and suitability should be explained by the clinician rather than assumed from spa advertising.

Does it treat sleep problems?

It may be discussed as a relaxation-oriented traditional practice; claims that it reliably treats a sleep disorder require evidence and proper medical evaluation.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
