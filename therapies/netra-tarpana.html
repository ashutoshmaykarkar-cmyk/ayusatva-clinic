Netra Tarpana

नेत्र तर्पण

A specialized classical eye-care practice

Netra Tarpana (Akshi Tarpana) is an Ayurvedic eye-related procedure traditionally involving retention of a selected medium around the eyes. Because the eye is particularly sensitive, it requires specialist screening and precise professional technique.

01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Netra Tarpana.

Netra Tarpana belongs to a family of traditional procedures described within Ayurvedic eye care. It is not equivalent to routine eye drops, and its historical use is not proof of modern clinical benefit. The clinician must determine whether any intervention is appropriate.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

Traditional eye-care information and questions about whether an eye-related procedure is suitable for a particular individual.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What a session may involve.

The following is a broad orientation, not a procedure manual. Exact methods differ across practitioners and patients.

01

Before the session

A qualified clinician reviews eye symptoms, diagnoses, contact lens use, allergies and infection risks before considering any therapy.

02

During the session

If appropriate, a trained professional manages the selected medium, positioning and aseptic safeguards.

03

Aftercare & review

The eyes are checked afterward, and the patient is told which symptoms require urgent ophthalmic review.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

Do not attempt any eye-retention procedure at home. Eye pain, redness, photophobia, loss of vision or injury warrants prompt ophthalmologist review. Contaminated products or improper technique can cause serious eye damage.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Is Netra Tarpana safe for anyone with dry eyes?

No. Dry-eye symptoms have many causes, and this procedure is not universally appropriate. An eye professional should establish the diagnosis and guide care.

Can it replace prescribed eye drops or ophthalmic care?

No. Do not stop eye medicines or postpone medical assessment to try a traditional treatment.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
