01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Basti.

Ayurvedic texts distinguish varieties of Basti such as Anuvasana and Niruha. Their classification and traditional descriptions should not be taken as a reason to attempt the procedure independently.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

An explanation of how Basti is classified in Panchakarma and why assessment is essential before considering any invasive procedure.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

This is an invasive procedure with potential harm, including irritation, infection, electrolyte disturbance or injury. People with gastrointestinal symptoms or other medical conditions require careful assessment. Never attempt a home or improvised Basti procedure.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Are all Basti procedures the same?

No. Different classical formulations and procedures are described, each requiring specialist judgment.

Is Basti safe as a general “detox”?

It should not be marketed or used as a universal detox. Any proposed clinical use requires informed discussion of risks and alternatives.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
