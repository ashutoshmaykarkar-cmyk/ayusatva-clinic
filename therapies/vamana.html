01 / THE TRADITION

Understanding Vamana.

Classical sources describe Vamana alongside careful preparation, patient selection and aftercare. Induced vomiting is medically consequential and can cause aspiration, dehydration, and electrolyte disturbances. General website information must not be treated as advice to undertake it.

Ayurvedic therapy choices are traditionally individualized. The description on this page is educational: it is not a diagnosis, a prescription or confirmation that this therapy is available at Ayusatva.

02 / IN CLINICAL CONVERSATION

When might it be discussed?

Why this procedure appears in classical descriptions, how clinicians assess contraindications, and why other medical options may be more appropriate.

A responsible conversation also considers current medicines, symptoms that warrant investigation, allergies, other care options, likely costs and expected follow-up. A treatment recommendation should be based on assessment rather than a promotional claim.

04 / SAFETY FIRST

Care with consideration.

Never induce vomiting at home for any wellness or “detox” purpose. Aspiration, airway injury, dehydration and electrolyte abnormalities are serious potential harms. Seek urgent medical help for uncontrolled vomiting or breathing difficulty.

Do not discontinue prescribed medicines or delay urgent medical care to try an Ayurvedic therapy. Seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional and ask about contraindications, consent, potential adverse effects and alternatives.

05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Can Vamana be done at home?

No. Do not self-induce vomiting or follow online recipes for emesis.

Is Vamana appropriate for general seasonal cleansing?

No procedure should be presented as universally indicated. Medical eligibility and alternatives must be assessed on an individual basis.

Does this page confirm the therapy is offered at Ayusatva?

No. This is a general education page. The clinic needs to verify its available services and determine suitability after an individual consultation.
